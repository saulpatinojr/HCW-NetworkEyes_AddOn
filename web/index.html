This tool reads one export of your Azure network resources. It never asks for credentials and never connects to your cloud. Everything is held in memory, is returned only to this browser session, and expires after two hours or when you delete it.

1. Upload a Resource Graph export

Accepted: the JSON from az graph query over microsoft.network/* (array or envelope) or the Resource Graph Explorer CSV. A portal "All resources" CSV is accepted too, with the rules that read properties reported as unknown. Up to 5 MB and 5,000 rows. The export commands are in the user guide; Reader on the subscriptions is enough.

        What this cannot tell you

        • Utilisation and saturation of gateways, firewalls, load balancers and circuits (metrics)
        • Diagnostic settings on firewalls and Bastion hosts; attacks in progress
        • BGP peer state, learned and advertised routes; effective routes and effective NSG rules per network interface
        • Private endpoint DNS resolution from inside the VNet; Defender assessments; cost
        • Flow logs, when the export carries no networkwatchers/flowlogs rows
        • Resources outside microsoft.network/, resources the exporting identity could not read, and subscriptions not in the export
